SHOE BANK - COMPLETE IMPLEMENTATION REPORT
Generated: 18 August 2026

===============================================================================
1. PROJECT AND ARCHITECTURE
===============================================================================

Shoe Bank is implemented as a PHP 8.1+ and MySQL/MariaDB mobile-first footwear
wholesale management system. Although the original request referred to MERN, the
actual repository is a lightweight custom PHP MVC application. The implemented
work respects the existing architecture instead of introducing a second stack.

Core technical foundation:
- Custom Router, Request, Controller, Session, Database and Model layers.
- PDO database access and database transactions for financial/inventory writes.
- Tailwind CSS UI, Alpine.js interactions and responsive mobile navigation.
- Session authentication, role/permission checks, CSRF protection and escaped
  view output.
- Validated local file storage for images/documents with thumbnail support.
- Audit logging for important business actions.

===============================================================================
2. PRODUCT CATALOGUE AND INVENTORY
===============================================================================

Implemented product functionality:
- Product create, view, update and controlled delete operations.
- Imported, local and custom product types.
- Art Number, product name, brand, category, colour, size set, purchase cost,
  selling price, stock and product image management.
- Search by Art Number, name, brand, category and colour.
- Automatic filters for type, stock state, brand and category, with Reset.
- Stock adjustment and stock-movement history.
- Product image upload, validation, thumbnails and missing-image reminders.
- Products without images remain visible and show an Add Image action.
- Fixed the product page rendering failure caused by a malformed category
  select element.
- Fixed paginated product counting so joined brand/category filters work.
- Added the missing Database::column() utility used by product forms.
- Product detail includes an explicit Delete action with soft-delete protection.
- Product and supplier-purchase forms use the same category-aware size-set list.
  Selecting a size set such as "Gents 7-10 (4 pr)" fills its category and pairs
  per set automatically.
- Supplier-invoice OCR product mapping is intentionally restricted to exactly:
  full Art Number, size set, colour, Indian MRP and pair count.
- OCR never invents a brand, derives pairs-per-set as a scanned product value, or
  stores the supplier rate/line amount as product data. If no explicit MRP is
  printed, Indian MRP remains blank for manual confirmation.

Variant grouping implemented:
- Existing database rows sharing the same Art Number and Category are presented
  as one base product card in the product list.
- Grouped cards show combined stock, available colours and related images.
- Search and pagination count the grouped catalogue correctly.
- Future confirmed purchase arrivals reuse an existing Art Number + Category
  base product when appropriate, while purchase-line colours remain traceable
  variants.

Inventory safety:
- Creating a purchase does not increase stock.
- Assigning clearance or marking a shipment in transit does not increase stock.
- Inventory increases only after arrival counting and final verification.
- Confirmed stock changes produce stock-history records.

Add Bill workflow:
- A dedicated /bills entry point first asks the user to select a customer.
- The bill form intentionally contains only customer, bill number, bill date,
  amount and an optional bill image.
- Technical/unnecessary note fields were removed from the normal workflow.
- Duplicate bill-number validation is enforced.
- Bill creation, ledger entry and customer outstanding update are performed in

  inconsistencies.
- The bill image is stored against the customer ledger transaction and can be
  viewed later from customer history.

Digital customer ledger:
- Append-only debit/credit transaction history with running balance.
- Bills and payments record separate business dates.
- Bill and cheque proof images are accessible from relevant ledger rows.
- Outstanding balance is updated transactionally with the ledger record.
- Existing bill and payment records have Edit actions. Corrections recalculate
  the full running ledger and current outstanding balance transactionally.
- Bounced/cancelled cheque-linked payments remain protected from unsafe edits.

4. PAYMENTS AND CHEQUES
===============================================================================

Simplified payment workflow:
- A dedicated /payments entry point first asks the user to select a customer.
- The normal payment form supports only Cash and Cheque, matching the requested
  shop workflow.
- Reference and general notes fields were removed from the normal form.
- Customer outstanding is shown prominently before payment.
- Recent customer ledger entries are displayed for context.
- A live calculation shows payment amount and remaining outstanding.
- Overpayments are rejected.
- Payment, ledger credit and outstanding reduction occur in one transaction
  while the customer row is locked.
- A completion receipt provides Share, Done and View Ledger actions.

Cheque workflow:
- Cheque fields appear only when Cheque is selected.
- Cheque number, bank, cheque date, payment date and optional cheque image are
  captured separately.
- Lifecycle supports Pending -> Bank Deposited -> Cleared or Bounced.
- Pending cheques can be cancelled under the existing transition rules.
- Bounce processing restores the customer outstanding through a ledger
  adjustment exactly once.
- Cheque list includes waiting, deposited, cleared and bounced states.
- Due/late warnings identify cheques that should be banked.
- Filter controls wrap correctly on mobile instead of producing a clipped
  horizontal strip.
- Payment history now links to the correct customer ledger.
- One customer payment can contain multiple cheques, each with its own cheque
  number, bank, amount, cheque date and proof image. Each cheque receives its own
  payment/cheque/ledger records while the screen validates the combined total.
- Repeatable cheque cards support adding and removing entries before saving.
- Cheque images can be read locally with Tesseract into an editable draft.

5. PURCHASE, CLEARANCE, PARCEL, ARRIVAL AND COSTING WORKFLOW
===============================================================================

Simplified purchase entry:
- The New Purchase page now provides one supplier-invoice uploader supporting
  PDF or invoice photos, plus a clear Manual Entry option.
- Removed the three confusing upload-type choices.
- Removed customer-facing API-key/technical configuration text.
- Removed the unrelated calculation-notes panel from purchase creation.
- Uploaded extraction remains a review step; it never adds stock directly.
- Manual invoice lines capture supplier/product/variant/quantity/cost data.
- Purchase list filters submit automatically.
- Local Tesseract OCR is the default free document reader. The Docker image now

- Printed multi-column supplier invoices use a table-oriented OCR layout mode.
- The supplied Mahashakti PDF was recognized as invoice CASH-525/26-27 with all
  16 product rows. The W 53200 / 08X10 example was mapped to Art Number
  "W 53200", size "8-10", colour "BLUE", Indian MRP 229 and 18 pairs.
- Invoice scanning creates an editable draft only. Confirming stores the
  purchase and purchase lines; product stock is created/updated only after
  arrival verification.
- One supplier invoice is scanned at a time, while the same clearance person can
  be assigned multiple purchases/invoices, each retained separately.

Purchase lifecycle:
- Draft -> Ordered -> Assigned -> In Transit -> Arrived -> Verification Pending
  -> Completed.
- Fixed the completed-purchase screen, which previously checked a non-existent
  "verified" status and displayed the wrong next actions.
- Completed purchases now show Step 7 of 7 and the landed-cost action when
  costing remains outstanding.
- Purchase edits are allowed only before the protected arrival/completion stage.
- Only safe Draft purchases can be deleted.
- Delete Purchase uses an explicit warning and confirmation.
- Completed purchases are immutable business/inventory history.

Clearance-person management:
- Clearance person create, view, update and safe deactivation/delete behaviour.
- Name, phone, active/inactive status, default rate and related shipment data.
- Call and WhatsApp actions on list and detail pages.
- Search/status filters update automatically.
- Cards show waiting parcels, active shipments, expected weight, expected pairs
  and verification progress.
- A clearance person cannot be deactivated while active assignments remain.
- Historical relationships are preserved.
- Detail screens show expected/arrived weight, expected pairs, verification,
  rate, clearance fee and payment status.
- Clearance payment can be marked Paid or Pending; paid date is retained.
- Each invoice has an informative verification summary containing supplier,
  invoice number/date, every product line, expected/received pairs, shortage,
  assigned/verified weight and verification status.
- Each invoice has a separate payment summary using the assignment's contracted
  weight, rate, amount and paid/pending state.
- Verification and payment summaries open as editable WhatsApp drafts for the
  particular clearance person. The app does not send without user confirmation.
- Past-shipment cards visibly show invoice identity, expected/received/shortage
  quantities, assigned/verified weight and clearance payment status.

Clearance assignments and parcel workflow:
- Multiple clearance people can be assigned to one purchase when a shipment is
  split.
- Each assignment stores weight, parcel count, rate, calculated cost, date,
  status and notes.
- Shipment weight reconciliation displays unassigned/excess weight warnings.
- A purchase cannot move to In Transit without a clearance assignment.
- Parcels support received/expected/damaged/missing status, given and arrived
  weights, carton count, date, remarks, clearance assignment and proof photo.
- Parcel records may be updated before verification is locked.
- After completion, UI actions for removing assignments, logging parcels and
  changing parcels are hidden.
- Controllers also reject direct attempts to change clearance assignments on a

Arrival verification:
- Final-count and incremental-count modes.
- Incremental entries retain count history and calculate received totals.
- Review supports matched product, new product, shortages, excess quantities,

- Final confirmation updates products/stock in a transaction and locks the
  inventory history against retrospective edits.

Landed costing:
- Discounted supplier amount and exchange conversion.
- Weight-per-pair and clearance-cost allocation.
- Handling cost, final landed cost and suggested selling price.
- Server-side CostCalculator keeps the official calculation consistent.

Legacy sales/invoice generator decision:
- The separate invoice-generator create workflow was removed from navigation

- Existing Sales history remains available as legacy/read-only business data.

10. IMPORTANT BUSINESS SAFETY RULES NOW ENFORCED
===============================================================================

- Imported goods are not added to sellable inventory before final verification.
- Only draft purchases may be deleted.
- Completed purchase, parcel, clearance and inventory history cannot be silently
  rewritten.
- Financial writes update ledger and customer outstanding transactionally.
- Overpayments are rejected.
- Pending/deposited cheques are receivables and are not treated as cleared cash.
- A bounced cheque restores customer debt only once.
- Bill and payment dates remain explicit business dates.
- Historical relationships are preserved when records are safely deactivated.
