SHOE BANK - IMPLEMENTATION REPORT
Generated: 16 August 2026

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------
Shoe Bank is a PHP 8.1+, MySQL/MariaDB mobile-first footwear wholesale
management system. It uses a lightweight custom MVC structure, Tailwind CSS
through CDN, Alpine.js, local file storage, session authentication, CSRF
protection, and role-based permissions. It is designed for cheap shared/cPanel
hosting without React, Laravel, or a Node build process.

2. IMPLEMENTED BUSINESS MODULES
-------------------------------

Authentication and access
- Session login/logout.
- Guest, authenticated-user, and administrator middleware.
- CSRF tokens on POST forms.
- Admin-only protection for sensitive destructive actions such as product,
  customer, and draft-purchase deletion.

Dashboard and navigation
- Mobile-oriented Home, Products, Customers, Finance, and More navigation.
- Floating quick-action menu for invoices, customers, products, and purchases.
- Dashboard cards and alerts for financial status, overdue customers, cheques,
  imports, arrivals, and stock conditions.

Products and inventory
- Imported, local, and custom products.
- Brands, categories, size sets, colours, Art Numbers, product images, selling
  prices, stock, and stock history.
- Product search and stock adjustment.
- Product image upload, validation, thumbnails, and safe local file storage.
- Local products do not use Indian landed-cost calculations.

Indian landed-cost calculation
- Shared server-side CostCalculator service.
- Discounted INR amount, LKR exchange conversion, Rs.25 rounding, weight per
  pair, pairs per kilogram, clearance cost per pair, handling charge, final
  landed cost, and suggested selling price.
- Cost calculations are kept on the backend rather than duplicated in browser
  JavaScript.
- Historical sale-item cost is snapshotted at the time of sale, so later
  re-costing does not change old profitability.

Customers, credit and ledger
- Customer create, update, soft delete, and restore.
- Customer search and mobile-friendly filtering.
- Customer detail dashboard with outstanding balance, ledger, bills, payments,
  invoices, cheques, and intelligence information.
- Manual historic/credit bills with a separately stored bill date.
- Append-only customer ledger transactions and running balance.
- Configurable default customer credit period.
- Customer intelligence based on actual purchase and payment history:
  reliable/good, slow/late, defaulter/risk, overdue, frequent, and inactive
  indicators.

Payments and cheques
- Cash, bank-transfer, cheque, and card payment recording.
- Partial payment support.
- Payment date stored separately from bill/invoice date.
- Customer payment, ledger entry, and outstanding-balance update are now
  executed inside one database transaction.
- Customer row locking prevents simultaneous submissions from using the same
  outstanding balance.
- Overpayments are rejected instead of silently creating inconsistent history.
- Cheque records support number, bank, amount, cheque date, deposit date,
  status, image, notes, and bounce reason.
- Cheque flow: Pending -> Deposited -> Cleared or Bounced; cancellation is
  allowed while pending.
- Deposited and pending cheques remain receivables. Only cleared cheques are
  counted as actual cash received.
- Bounce creates a ledger adjustment that restores the customer outstanding
  balance exactly once.
- Cheque reminders and dashboard/list warnings for cheques due for banking.
- New database migration: database/migrations/005_cheque_deposited_status.sql
  adds the deposited cheque status to existing installations.

Sales and finance
- Wholesale/retail sales, cash/credit sales, invoice lines, and stock deduction.
- Credit sales create customer ledger debt; cash sales record counter payment.
- Sales are transactional across invoice, line items, stock movement, and
  customer ledger changes.
- Sale cancellation restores stock while retaining invoice history.
- Expenses are separate from inventory purchases.
- Finance views distinguish revenue/profit from cash collection/receivables.
- Profit calculations use sales revenue, snapshotted COGS, clearance costs, and
  operating expenses.
- Reports include stock, imports, clearance, costs, and receivables.

Import purchase, clearance, parcel, and arrival workflow
- Purchase entry via manual entry, scanned image, PDF, handwritten invoice, or
  an editable OCR/extraction result.
- OCR/extraction never creates inventory automatically; the owner reviews and
  confirms all extracted data first.
- Purchase line items include brand, Art Number, colour, size set, pairs per
  set, quantity, INR price, and totals.
- Existing products are matched using Art Number, brand, and colour to avoid
  accidental variant collisions.
- Many-to-many Purchase <-> Clearance Person assignments.
- Each clearance assignment stores assigned weight, parcel count, rate per kg,
  calculated clearance cost, date, status, and notes.
- Shipment weight reconciliation compares total shipment weight with allocated
  clearance weight.
- A shipment cannot be marked In Transit without a clearance assignment.
- Parcels support expected/received/damaged/missing states, weights, carton
  counts, arrival date, notes, responsible clearance assignment, and photo
  evidence.
- Parcel photo uploads are validated and stored correctly as purchase
  attachments. This corrected a previous runtime-breaking storage-method call.
- Existing parcel records can be updated from the purchase screen until the
  arrival is confirmed.
- Purchase edit/update is available before arrival; it safely replaces invoice
  lines and re-runs product matching in one transaction.
- Draft purchases can be deleted by an administrator. Dispatch/arrival history
  is deliberately retained and cannot be deleted.
- Arrival verification has final-count and incremental-count modes.
- Incremental counts create count history and the received total is calculated
  automatically.
- Inventory is not increased when a purchase is created, assigned, in transit,
  or merely arrived.
- Inventory increases only after verification confirmation, inside a database
  transaction, and creates stock-history records.
- Confirmed arrivals lock parcel history so the audit trail and inventory cannot
  be changed retrospectively.
- Arrival verification supports matched, shortage, excess, partial receipt,
  parcel/weight reconciliation, and final confirmation.
- Landed costing is available after arrival confirmation.

Attachments and uploads
- Supplier invoices, handwritten notes, clearance documents, delivery receipts,
  parcel proof, calculation notes, and other documents.
- File MIME/type and size validation.
- Safe generated filenames, protected upload location, and image thumbnails.
- Standalone calculation notes can later be attached to a purchase.

Settings and supporting operations
- Configurable business/cost/credit/reminder settings.
- Customer-intelligence recomputation.
- Overdue reminder scripts/cron entry points.
- Storage/log/backups folder structure.

6. INTENTIONAL SAFETY RULES
---------------------------
- Imported goods cannot be sold from this workflow until arrival verification
  has confirmed inventory.
- Confirmed inventory and parcel history are not silently rewritten.
- Only draft purchases may be deleted; other purchases remain as business
  history.
- Pending/deposited cheques are not cash collection.
- Old sale profit is not changed by later product re-costing.
- Local products do not receive Indian import cost treatment.
